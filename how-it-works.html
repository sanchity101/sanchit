Setting up your secure cloud storage takes just a few minutes. Follow these simple steps to ensure your files are backed up and safe forever.

Create Your Account

Begin by signing up for a free CloudVault account. You only need a valid email address and a strong password to get started.

Upload Your Files

Navigate to your dashboard and simply drag and drop your files. You can upload documents, images, videos, and entire folders with full folder structure preservation.

Automatic Encryption

This step happens entirely in the background. Before your files are even fully uploaded, CloudVault encrypts them using AES-256 standards, ensuring total privacy.

Manage & Access Anywhere

Organize your files into folders, share them securely via links, or access them from your mobile device. Your digital life is now perfectly synced.
