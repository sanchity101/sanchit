How CloudVault Works

From the moment you sign up to uploading, encrypting, and accessing your files across devices — simple, fast, and completely secure.

Create & Secure Your Account

Sign up with your email and establish your unique master credentials or hardware passkey. Your cryptographic keys are initialized locally to ensure zero-knowledge privacy.

Upload Files with Drag & Drop

Simply drag documents, images, videos, or archives straight into your web browser or dedicated app. Files are automatically chunked and encrypted before transmission.

Your files are stored across fault-tolerant, geo-redundant storage clusters with 99.999999999% durability. Automated backup guarantees your data is never lost.
